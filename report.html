How to read these results

Method & environment

Read the production bug-bash checklist and the browser kit README, handoff, validation, fixture instructions, and relevant implementation. Created a dedicated clone named Scratch Card QA - 2026-11-05. Builder checks used the authenticated account. Storefront checks used fresh Playwright contexts with pinned Chromium 153.0.8010.12 and real pointer/keyboard/touch input.

Acceptance runs loaded production signup_forms, Render, and full-forms responses. They used no full-forms fixtures, response mocks, or assetSource parameter. See observed production script/response URLs (HTTP 200). The dedicated test browser used the kit-compatible LocalNetworkAccessChecks launch flag for this machine’s network bootstrap. Mobile and reduced-motion coverage used browser emulation.

The QA form was custom-trigger-only and opened through _klOnsite.push(['openForm', 'StWjtg']). Existing unrelated automatic popups were present on this test store; longer reruns dismissed reachable unrelated dialogs through their UI controls. Results affected by those popups were superseded, not counted as feature defects.

A fresh builder load changed the observed controls and foil rendering. Prompt fitting, styles, width, and foil were rechecked against the refreshed UI and live assets. Earlier missing-control/texture observations are not reported as current failures.

Final form: custom trigger only, 50% threshold, full width, height 160px, radius 28px, blue foil, IBM Plex Mono 24px bold italic yellow prompt, and Do nothing on reveal. It remains published for inspection. Other forms were not edited.
